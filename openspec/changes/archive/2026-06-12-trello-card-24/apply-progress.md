## Summary

Fixed the false negative in `worktree-cleanup.sh` where the `is_merged` heuristic only checked the exact `--base` ref, missing merge evidence available on upstream or remote-tracking refs. Added four helpers (`debug_log`, `resolve_base_candidates`, `candidate_has_merged_tip`, `candidate_has_patch_equivalence`) and replaced the `is_merged` body with a two-pass candidate loop (ancestry first, patch-id second). Added 5 new hermetic tests covering remote merge detection, rebase patch-id, branch-ahead safety, remote-deleted branches, and conflict-resolution merges.
